i have realized i spend too much time optimizing my work and not enough time optimizing my life. 

here's a simple plan for next year:

- **reading fantasy:** my friends have stopped asking for book recommendations because they hate my "intellectual" non-fiction picks. i am finally picking up brandon sanderson to see why everyone loves magic and dragons more than reality.

- **walking & muscle:** every time bangalore's weather turns bad, i fall sick. it is time to build some muscle and walk 10k steps daily so a cloudy day doesn't defeat me.

- **home food:** eating out is hurting my wallet and my cholesterol. eating at home saves money, and honestly, chopping vegetables with my wife is better than waiting for a delivery guy.

- **phone detox:** i spend 6 hours a day doom scrolling. that is basically a full-time job for free. i need to put the phone down and get my brain back.
